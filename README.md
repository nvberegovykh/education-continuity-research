# Coordinated Development and Independent Living

**A critical review and research protocol for the 3–4–3–4–3–4–2 model**  
Nikita Beregovykh · Version 1.0 · 2 November 2026

[Read on Research.LIBER](https://research.liberpict.com/education-continuity/) · [Native research record](https://research.liberpict.com/#/r/education-continuity) · [Read the paper on GitHub](manuscript.md)

This paper develops a proposal for consistent coordination across childcare, education, practical projects, professional preparation, housing and healthcare access, and independent adult life. It includes comparisons with Finland, Japan, Montessori, Swiss vocational training, US Career Academies, project-based learning trials, and integrated student supports, with 20 references and four proposed studies.

The publication is a **targeted narrative review and research proposal**, not a completed participant study, peer-reviewed result, or demonstration of genetic stage lengths. No participant data are included. The exact durations end at the 23rd birthday; the initiating completed-age labels form a separate 24-year variant.

Compulsory developmental monitoring ends at legal adulthood in the proposed policy, using 18 only as a reference implementation. Later support and research follow-up are optional. Family planning is independent of educational progress, tests, income, and sponsor approval. Ages 27–30 are an optional retreat and reassessment window; no universal optimal reproductive age or retreat age is established.
